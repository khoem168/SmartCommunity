# Section 01 — Project Introduction, Vision & Problem Statement

Presentation-ready content for the **Smart Community Skill & Career Development Platform**.

## Verified project scope

The following presentation content is based on features that exist in the codebase:

- React 19 + Vite user interface with responsive layout and bilingual support (Khmer/English).
- Node.js + Express REST API with MySQL/MariaDB data storage.
- JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and role-based access control (Student, Mentor, Recruiter, Admin).
- Unified ecosystem integrating Courses, Mentorship, Peer Skill Exchange, Career Roadmaps, and Internships.
- Real-time communication via Socket.IO, live meetings via Jitsi Meet, and course payments via Bakong KHQR.

## Slide 02 — Problem Statement & Local Context

### The Challenge Facing Cambodian Learners & Job Seekers

#### 01 — Fragmented Digital Tools
Learning resources, mentor advice, portfolio records, and job listings are scattered across Facebook groups, Telegram channels, and separate web portals, causing learners to lose track of progress.

#### 02 — Lack of Direct Peer Collaboration
Students frequently lack opportunities to practice skills collaboratively or trade complementary knowledge (e.g., UI/UX design for JavaScript coding) without paying expensive fees.

#### 03 — Limited Guidance & Mentorship Access
Emerging talent outside major hubs struggles to connect with verified industry professionals for 1:1 portfolio reviews and career direction.

#### 04 — The Skill-to-Job Gap
Graduates have theoretical knowledge but lack clear milestone roadmaps that connect their coursework directly to verified employer internship requirements.

## Slide 03 — Our Solution: The Smart Community Platform

### A Unified, Role-Based Skill & Career Acceleration Ecosystem

```
   [ Courses & KHQR ]  ──┐
   [ Peer Skill Swap ]  ──┼─► [ Role-Adaptive Workspace ] ─► [ Career Bridge ] ─► [ Verified Jobs ]
   [ 1:1 Mentorship  ]  ──┤
   [ Live Jitsi Hub  ]  ──┘
```

#### 01 — Learn & Upskill
Access structured video/reading modules in Khmer & English. Seamlessly unlock premium courses using Cambodia's national standard Bakong KHQR.

#### 02 — Collaborate & Exchange
Post skills you can teach and skills you want to learn. Our automated compatibility engine matches peer students for mutual knowledge exchange.

#### 03 — Connect & Get Guided
Book verified mentors for 1:1 guidance sessions and join live interactive learning workshops directly integrated with Jitsi Meet.

#### 04 — Career Roadmaps to Verified Jobs
Complete career assessments, generate visual skill roadmaps, and bridge course achievements directly into verified internship applications.

## Likely teacher questions and clear answers

### 1. What motivated your team to build this project?
Many students in Cambodia learn theoretical concepts in universities but face difficulties finding practical projects, mentors, or internships. We wanted to build a single platform where learning directly translates into real-world career opportunities.

### 2. Can high school students or non-university learners use this platform?
Yes. Anyone can register as a student. All they need is an email account. Courses, forums, and skill exchange are open to any self-taught individual or student.
